Why Health Amplified?

Because nobody understands youth health challenges better than, well… youth. In six fast‑paced weeks you’ll team‑up, research what really matters, and pitch fixes to the adults who can make them happen.

🚀 Quick Mission Brief

Power up a peer‑education crew, run a youth‑led needs check, and rally schools + community partners to smash health gaps across Solano County.

🛠️ What You’ll Tackle

  • Boost on‑campus health pop‑ups & events
  • Run listening sessions + surveys 🔍
  • Dig into real‑world health data
  • Create scroll‑stopping IG/TikTok health posts
  • Co‑design solutions with pros & policymakers
  • Pitch your ideas at a county‑level showcase

✨ Why It Rocks

  • Earn a $1000 stipend
  • Your ideas guide REAL policy moves
  • Meet mentors & leaders across health fields
  • Help friends & fam access better care
  • Leave a blueprint future interns can build on
“I signed up for the internship to get hours for college apps—ended up changing how my school talks about mental health.” – 2024 intern

Your 6‑Week Adventure

Level‑up week by week. Here’s the game plan:

Week 1

Systems Scavenger Hunt

Map every health resource (or gap) around your campus & city.

Week 2

Listening Toolkit

Build surveys, vox‑pop questions & interview skills that get honest answers.

Week 3

Campus Takeover

Host peer events + collect stories that matter.

Week 4

Data Detective

Turn raw feedback into clear trends & eye‑opening stats.

Week 5

Solution Sprint

Co‑design fresh ideas with experts & test them for fit.

Week 6

Big‑Stage Showcase

Pitch to school boards, clinics & county reps—secure commitments.
